## Set P3: Random variables, expectation and variance

### Easy

**P3.E1** · MCQ · 1 mark. X takes the values 1, 2 and 3 with probabilities 0.2, 0.5 and 0.3. E[X] is (A) 2.0 (B) 2.1 (C) 2.3 (D) 1.9

**P3.E2** · NAT · 1 mark. For the X of P3.E1, find Var(X). (Round off to two decimal places)

**P3.E3** · MCQ · 1 mark. If Var(X) = 4, then Var(3X + 2) is (A) 36 (B) 12 (C) 14 (D) 38

**P3.E4** · NAT · 1 mark. f(x) = cx² for 0 ≤ x ≤ 2 (and 0 elsewhere) is a probability density function. Find c. (Round off to three decimal places)

**P3.E5** · MSQ · 1 mark. Which properties does every cumulative distribution function F have? (A) F is continuous (B) F(x) → 1 as x → −∞ (C) F is non-decreasing (D) F is right-continuous

**P3.E6** · MCQ · 1 mark. The expected value of the score of one roll of a fair six-sided die is (A) 3 (B) 4 (C) 3.5 (D) 6

**P3.E7** · MCQ · 1 mark. If E[X] = 2 and E[X²] = 13, the standard deviation of X is (A) 3 (B) 9 (C) 11 (D) √13

**P3.E8** · NAT · 1 mark. X has density f(x) = 2x on [0, 1]. Find P(X ≤ 0.5). (Round off to two decimal places)

### Medium

**P3.M1** · MSQ · 2 marks. X has density f(x) = 2x on [0, 1]. Which statements are correct? (A) The median of X is 1/√2 (B) P(X ≤ 1/2) = 1/2 (C) E[X] = 2/3 (D) Var(X) = 1/18

**P3.M2** · MCQ · 2 marks. X has CDF F(x) = x³ on [0, 1]. Its median is closest to (A) 0.50 (B) 0.71 (C) 0.79 (D) 0.88

**P3.M3** · NAT · 2 marks. A discrete X has CDF F with F(x) = 0 for x < 0, 0.2 for 0 ≤ x < 1, 0.5 for 1 ≤ x < 2, 0.9 for 2 ≤ x < 3 and 1 for x ≥ 3. Find P(1 ≤ X ≤ 2). (Round off to one decimal place)

**P3.M4** · NAT · 2 marks. X takes non-negative integer values with P(X ≥ k) = (2/3)ᵏ for k = 0, 1, 2, …. Find E[X].

**P3.M5** · MCQ · 2 marks. X is uniform on {−2, −1, 0, 1, 2} and Y = X². E[Y] is (A) 1 (B) 0 (C) 2 (D) 4

**P3.M6** · MSQ · 2 marks. X has density f(x) = 3x²/8 on [0, 2]. Which are correct? (A) E[X] = 1.5 (B) The median of X is 1.5 (C) The median of X is greater than its mean (D) Var(X) = 0.25

**P3.M7** · MSQ · 2 marks. A game costs ₹10. You roll a fair die and win ₹5 times the face value if the face is 4 or more, and nothing otherwise. Which statements are correct? (A) P(winning something) = 1/3 (B) The expected net gain is ₹2.5 (C) The game is unfavourable to the player (D) The expected winnings are ₹15

**P3.M8** · MCQ · 2 marks. X has mean μ and variance σ². The constant c that minimises E[(X − c)²] is (A) μ (B) the median of X (C) the mode of X (D) 0

**P3.M9** · NAT · 2 marks. X has variance 4. Find the upper bound that Chebyshev's inequality gives for P(|X − μ| ≥ 4). (Round off to two decimal places)

### Hard

**P3.H1** · NAT · 2 marks. A fair die is rolled until every face has appeared at least once. Find the expected number of rolls. (Round off to one decimal place)

**P3.H2** · NAT · 2 marks. Two fair dice are rolled. Find the expected value of the larger of the two scores. (Round off to two decimal places)

**P3.H3** · MCQ · 2 marks. X has density f(x) = e^(−|x|)/2 for all real x. Var(X) is (A) 2 (B) 1 (C) 1/2 (D) 4

**P3.H4** · NAT · 2 marks. X has CDF F(x) = 0 for x < 0, x/4 for 0 ≤ x < 2, and 1 for x ≥ 2. Find E[X]. (Round off to one decimal place)

**P3.H5** · MSQ · 2 marks. X is a non-negative random variable with E[X] = 2. Which statements are always true? (A) P(X ≥ 6) ≤ 1/3 (B) P(X ≥ 1) ≥ 1/2 (C) E[√X] ≥ √2 (D) E[X²] ≥ 4

**P3.H6** · NAT · 2 marks. Ten hats are returned to their ten owners in a uniformly random order. Find the expected number of people who get their own hat.

**P3.H7** · MSQ · 2 marks. A stick of length 1 is broken at a uniformly random point U. Which statements are correct? (A) The expected ratio longer/shorter is finite (B) The longer piece is uniform on [1/2, 1] (C) The expected length of the shorter piece is 1/4 (D) The expected length of the longer piece is 3/4

## Set P4: Joint behaviour, covariance, conditional expectation and descriptive statistics

### Easy

**P4.E1** · MCQ · 1 mark. Cov(X, Y) = 3, Var(X) = 4 and Var(Y) = 9. The correlation coefficient is (A) 1/12 (B) 0.25 (C) 3 (D) 0.5

**P4.E2** · NAT · 1 mark. With the values of P4.E1, find Var(X + Y).

**P4.E3** · MCQ · 1 mark. X and Y are independent with Var(X) = 2 and Var(Y) = 3. Var(2X − Y) is (A) 11 (B) 5 (C) 1 (D) 7

**P4.E4** · NAT · 1 mark. Find the population variance (dividing by n) of the data 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9.

**P4.E5** · MSQ · 1 mark. Which statements are true for random variables with finite variance? (A) Cov(X, X) = Var(X) (B) If X and Y are independent, Cov(X, Y) = 0 (C) If Cov(X, Y) = 0, X and Y are independent (D) The correlation coefficient lies in [0, 1]

**P4.E6** · NAT · 1 mark. Find the median of 3, 8, 1, 9, 4, 7. (Round off to one decimal place)

**P4.E7** · MCQ · 1 mark. For random variables X and Y, E[E[X | Y]] equals (A) E[Y] (B) E[X] (C) E[X | Y] (D) E[XY]

**P4.E8** · MCQ · 1 mark. Cov(X, Y) = 1.5. Cov(2X + 1, 3Y − 4) is (A) 9 (B) 4.5 (C) 1.5 (D) 10

### Medium

**P4.M1** · NAT · 2 marks. X and Y take values 0 and 1 with P(0, 0) = 0.2, P(0, 1) = 0.3, P(1, 0) = 0.1 and P(1, 1) = 0.4. Find Cov(X, Y). (Round off to two decimal places)

**P4.M2** · MCQ · 2 marks. For the joint distribution of P4.M1, P(Y = 1 | X = 1) is (A) 0.4 (B) 0.8 (C) 0.57 (D) 0.7

**P4.M3** · NAT · 2 marks. X is uniform on (0, 1) and, given X = x, Y is uniform on (0, x). Find E[Y]. (Round off to two decimal places)

**P4.M4** · MSQ · 2 marks. X is uniform on (0, 1) and, given X = x, Y is uniform on (0, x). Which statements are correct? (A) E[Y | X] = X/2 (B) Var(Y) = 7/144 (C) E[Y] = 1/4 (D) Var(Y) = Var(X)/4

**P4.M5** · MSQ · 2 marks. X is uniform on [−1, 1] and Y = X². Which are correct? (A) The correlation of X and Y is 1 (B) E[Y] = 1/3 (C) X and Y are independent (D) Cov(X, Y) = 0

**P4.M6** · MSQ · 2 marks. The number of orders N in a day has mean 5 and variance 2. Each order's value has mean 3 and variance 4, independently of the others and of N. For the day's total T, which statements are correct? (A) Var(T) = 38 (B) If N were fixed at 5, Var(T) would be 38 (C) Var(T) = 5 × 4 + 2 × 3 (D) E[T] = 15

**P4.M7** · MCQ · 2 marks. Data with mean 20 and standard deviation 4 are transformed by y = 3x − 5. The new mean and standard deviation are (A) 55 and 12 (B) 60 and 12 (C) 55 and 7 (D) 55 and 36

**P4.M8** · NAT · 2 marks. One class of 10 students has mean mark 5 and another of 15 students has mean mark 10. Find the mean mark of all 25 students.

**P4.M9** · NAT · 2 marks. Y = −2X + 3 and Var(X) > 0. Find the correlation coefficient of X and Y.

### Hard

**P4.H1** · MSQ · 2 marks. X and Y are independent and uniform on (0, 1). Which statements are correct? (A) E[max] + E[min] = 1 (B) E[max(X, Y)] = 2/3 (C) max(X, Y) and min(X, Y) are independent (D) E[min(X, Y)] = 1/3

**P4.H2** · NAT · 2 marks. X and Y are independent and uniform on (0, 1). Find E[|X − Y|]. (Round off to two decimal places)

**P4.H3** · MCQ · 2 marks. X and Y are independent with E[X] = 1, Var(X) = 1, E[Y] = 2 and Var(Y) = 3. Var(XY) is (A) 14 (B) 3 (C) 10 (D) 7

**P4.H4** · NAT · 2 marks. For 20 numbers x₁, …, x₂₀, Σᵢ Σⱼ (xᵢ − xⱼ)² = 1600, summing over all ordered pairs (i, j). Find the population variance (1/20) Σ (xᵢ − x̄)².

**P4.H5** · MSQ · 2 marks. Which statements are true for all random variables X and Y with finite variance? (A) Var(E[X | Y]) ≤ Var(X) (B) E[X | Y] is a constant (C) |Cov(X, Y)| ≤ σₓσᵧ (D) Var(X + Y) ≤ σₓ² + σᵧ²

**P4.H6** · NAT · 2 marks. X is uniform on (0, 1), Z is standard normal and independent of X, and Y = X + Z. Find the correlation coefficient of X and Y. (Round off to two decimal places)

**P4.H7** · NAT · 2 marks. (X, Y) has joint density f(x, y) = x + y on the unit square 0 ≤ x, y ≤ 1. Find Cov(X, Y). (Round off to four decimal places)
