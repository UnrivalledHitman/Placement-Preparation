# How to use this book
%General Aptitude practice, three full mock papers in the exact GATE format, and a score sheet

General Aptitude carries 15 of the 100 marks and is the same for every GATE paper: five one-mark and five two-mark questions on verbal ability, quantitative aptitude, and analytical and spatial reasoning. It rewards speed and care more than knowledge, so practise it all year in short sessions. The quantitative and verbal books elsewhere in this repository teach the methods; this book drills the GATE style.

The three **mock papers** follow the exam exactly: 65 questions for 100 marks in 3 hours. Q1–5 (1 mark) and Q6–10 (2 marks) are General Aptitude; Q11–35 (1 mark) and Q36–65 (2 marks) are the DA subject. Subject marks are split by the 2025–26 weightage: probability and statistics 19, linear algebra 10, calculus 6, programming and algorithms 14, databases and warehousing 14, machine learning 14, AI 8.

**How to sit a mock.** Take it in one 3-hour sitting with only a basic calculator. Mark with GATE rules: MCQs lose 1/3 (one-mark) or 2/3 (two-mark) when wrong; MSQs and NATs lose nothing. Then spend at least as long reviewing as you did writing: for every lost mark, note the topic and the cause (concept, calculation, misreading, time) on the score sheet.

# General Aptitude practice

## Set G1: Verbal ability

### Easy

**G1.E1** · MCQ · 1 mark. Thermometer : Temperature :: Odometer : ________ (A) Speed (B) Pressure (C) Distance (D) Time

**G1.E2** · MCQ · 1 mark. Which word is closest in meaning to "candid"? (A) Sweet (B) Frank (C) Hidden (D) Careful

**G1.E3** · MCQ · 1 mark. Neither the manager nor the employees ________ aware of the change. (A) was (B) were (C) is (D) has been

**G1.E4** · MCQ · 1 mark. Which word is most nearly opposite in meaning to "frugal"? (A) Thrifty (B) Careful (C) Extravagant (D) Poor

**G1.E5** · MCQ · 1 mark. She has been working here ________ 2019. (A) for (B) from (C) since (D) by

### Medium

**G1.M1** · MCQ · 2 marks. The number of applicants ________ risen sharply, and a number of them ________ already been called for interviews. (A) has, has (B) have, has (C) has, have (D) have, have

**G1.M2** · MCQ · 2 marks. Arrange the sentences into a coherent paragraph. P: It was therefore postponed by a week. Q: The match was scheduled for Saturday. R: However, heavy rain flooded the ground on Friday. S: The new date suits both teams. (A) RQPS (B) QPRS (C) QRPS (D) QRSP

**G1.M3** · MCQ · 2 marks. "Every successful startup in the survey had a clear revenue model, but some startups with a clear revenue model failed." Which conclusion follows? (A) In the survey, a clear revenue model was necessary but not sufficient for success (B) A clear revenue model guarantees success (C) Most surveyed startups failed (D) The failed startups had no revenue model

**G1.M4** · MCQ · 2 marks. Which word does not belong with the others? (A) Brisk (B) Sluggish (C) Swift (D) Rapid

**G1.M5** · MSQ · 2 marks. Which sentences use "affect" or "effect" correctly? (A) Stress can affect health. (B) The new policy will affect all employees. (C) The medicine had no effect. (D) The rain effected the match badly.

### Hard

**G1.H1** · MCQ · 2 marks. "Remote work removes commuting time, yet many firms report weaker collaboration. Teams that share two office days a week recover most of the collaboration while keeping most of the time saved." Which inference is best supported? (A) A hybrid schedule can balance time savings and collaboration (B) Fully remote work maximises collaboration (C) Commuting improves productivity (D) Offices should be closed

**G1.H2** · MCQ · 2 marks. Author : Manuscript :: Composer : ________ (A) Orchestra (B) Concert (C) Melody (D) Score

**G1.H3** · MSQ · 2 marks. Which words are spelt correctly? (A) separate (B) accommodate (C) definately (D) occurence

## Set G2: Quantitative aptitude

### Easy

**G2.E1** · MCQ · 1 mark. A price rises by 20% and then falls by 20%. The net change is (A) 0% (B) +4% (C) −4% (D) −2%

**G2.E2** · NAT · 1 mark. Find the average of the first 10 odd natural numbers.

**G2.E3** · MCQ · 1 mark. ₹1200 is divided in the ratio 3 : 5. The larger share is (A) ₹450 (B) ₹750 (C) ₹720 (D) ₹800

**G2.E4** · MCQ · 1 mark. A car covers 150 km in 2.5 hours. Its average speed is (A) 75 km/h (B) 50 km/h (C) 65 km/h (D) 60 km/h

**G2.E5** · NAT · 1 mark. Find the simple interest in rupees on ₹5000 at 8% per year for 3 years.

### Medium

**G2.M1** · MCQ · 2 marks. A finishes a job in 12 days and B in 18 days. Working together they finish it in (A) 9 days (B) 15 days (C) 6 days (D) 7.2 days

**G2.M2** · NAT · 2 marks. Two fair dice are thrown. Find the probability that the sum is at least 10. (Round off to two decimal places)

**G2.M3** · MCQ · 2 marks. The next number in 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, … is (A) 40 (B) 42 (C) 36 (D) 44

**G2.M4** · NAT · 2 marks. Find the compound interest in rupees on ₹10,000 at 10% per year, compounded yearly, for 2 years.

**G2.M5** · MCQ · 2 marks. Product X sold 120, 150, 180 and 210 units in quarters Q1 to Q4. The percentage increase from Q1 to Q4 is (A) 75% (B) 90% (C) 57% (D) 60%

**G2.M6** · MCQ · 2 marks. How many litres of water must be added to 20 litres of a 30% acid solution to make it 20% acid? (A) 12 (B) 5 (C) 8 (D) 10

### Hard

**G2.H1** · NAT · 2 marks. Trains of lengths 200 m and 300 m run in opposite directions at 72 km/h and 54 km/h. How many seconds do they take to pass each other completely? (Round off to two decimal places)

**G2.H2** · MCQ · 2 marks. The minimum value of f(x) = |x − 2| + |x + 3| over real x is (A) 0 (B) 5 (C) 1 (D) 3

**G2.H3** · MCQ · 2 marks. How many three-digit numbers are divisible by 7? (A) 128 (B) 129 (C) 127 (D) 143

**G3.E3** · MCQ · 1 mark. A person walks 3 km north and then 4 km east. How far is the person from the start? (A) 1 km (B) 7 km (C) 5 km (D) 12 km

**G3.E4** · MCQ · 1 mark. A is B's sister, C is B's mother, and D is C's father. How is A related to D? (A) Granddaughter (B) Daughter (C) Niece (D) Sister

**G3.E5** · MCQ · 1 mark. 1 January of a non-leap year is a Monday. 1 January of the next year is a (A) Monday (B) Tuesday (C) Wednesday (D) Sunday

### Medium

**G3.M1** · MCQ · 2 marks. P, Q, R, S and T sit in a row. S is in the middle seat; R is at one end and to the left of S; Q is immediately to the right of P; T sits next to S. Who sits at the right end? (A) T (B) P (C) R (D) Q

**G3.M2** · MCQ · 2 marks. A cube painted on every face is cut into 27 equal small cubes. How many small cubes have paint on exactly two faces? (A) 12 (B) 8 (C) 6 (D) 24

**G3.M3** · NAT · 2 marks. How many squares of all sizes are there in a 4 × 4 grid of unit squares?

**G3.M4** · MCQ · 2 marks. The angle between the hour and minute hands at 3:40 is (A) 110° (B) 120° (C) 140° (D) 130°

**G3.M5** · MSQ · 2 marks. "Some managers are engineers. All engineers are graduates." Which conclusions follow? (A) Some managers are graduates (B) No manager is a graduate (C) All graduates are engineers (D) Some graduates are managers

### Hard

**G3.H1** · MCQ · 2 marks. The faces of a cube are red, blue, green, yellow, white and black. Red is opposite blue and green is opposite white. Which colour is opposite yellow? (A) Red (B) Black (C) Green (D) White

**G3.H2** · NAT · 2 marks. Twelve people each shake hands once with every other person. How many handshakes take place?

**G3.H3** · MCQ · 2 marks. The mirror image of an analogue clock shows 4:20. The actual time is (A) 7:20 (B) 8:40 (C) 7:40 (D) 8:20
