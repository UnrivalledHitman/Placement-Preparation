## Set L2: Eigenvalues and eigenvectors

### Easy

**L2.E1** · MCQ · 1 mark. The eigenvalues of [[2, 0], [0, 5]] are (A) 0 and 7 (B) 2 and 5 (C) 10 and 1 (D) 2 and 0

**L2.E2** · MCQ · 1 mark. A 3 × 3 matrix has eigenvalues 1, 2 and 3. Its trace is (A) 3 (B) 6 (C) 1 (D) 9

**L2.E3** · MCQ · 1 mark. If 3 is an eigenvalue of A, then an eigenvalue of A² is (A) 3 (B) 6 (C) √3 (D) 9

**L2.E4** · NAT · 1 mark. A matrix has eigenvalues 2, −1 and 4. Find its determinant.

**L2.E5** · MSQ · 1 mark. Which real matrices always have only real eigenvalues? (A) Orthogonal matrices (B) Symmetric matrices (C) All 2 × 2 matrices (D) Upper triangular matrices

**L2.E6** · NAT · 1 mark. Find the larger eigenvalue of [[4, 1], [2, 3]].

**L2.E7** · MCQ · 1 mark. A is invertible and 4 is an eigenvalue of A. Then A⁻¹ has the eigenvalue (A) −4 (B) 4 (C) 1/4 (D) 16

**L2.E8** · NAT · 1 mark. Find the product of the eigenvalues of [[1, 2], [2, 1]].

### Medium

**L2.M1** · NAT · 2 marks. A is a 4 × 4 matrix with A² = A and rank 2. Find trace(A).

**L2.M2** · MCQ · 2 marks. The eigenvalues of [[0, 1], [−1, 0]] are (A) 1 and −1 (B) 1 and 1 (C) i and −i (D) 0 and 1

**L2.M3** · MSQ · 2 marks. A is 3 × 3 with eigenvalues 1, 2 and 3. Which statements are correct? (A) trace(A²) = 12 (B) det(A² + I) = 100 (C) A is singular (D) det(A − 4I) = 0

**L2.M4** · MSQ · 2 marks. A is a real symmetric 3 × 3 matrix with eigenvalues 0, 2 and 5. Which are correct? (A) A is positive semidefinite (B) rank(A) = 2 (C) A is invertible (D) trace(A) = 10

**L2.M5** · NAT · 2 marks. A = [[2, 1], [1, 2]] and v = (1, 1)ᵀ. Find the first component of A¹⁰v.

**L2.M6** · MCQ · 2 marks. A is a 3 × 3 real matrix with A³ = A. Which must be true? (A) det A ∈ {−1, 0, 1} (B) A is invertible (C) trace(A) = 0 (D) A = I

**L2.M7** · NAT · 2 marks. J is the 4 × 4 matrix of all ones. How many distinct eigenvalues does J have?

**L2.M8** · MSQ · 2 marks. A is 2 × 2 with trace 5 and determinant 6. Which statements are correct? (A) The eigenvalues of A² sum to 13 (B) trace(A⁻¹) = 1/5 (C) det(A²) = 36 (D) The eigenvalues of A are 2 and 3

**L2.M9** · MCQ · 2 marks. B = P⁻¹AP for an invertible P. Then A and B always have (A) the same eigenvectors (B) the same eigenvalues (C) B = A (D) B = Aᵀ

### Hard

**L2.H1** · NAT · 2 marks. B = [[1, 0, 0], [0, cos θ, −sin θ], [0, sin θ, cos θ]]. Find the smallest θ in (0°, 180°], in degrees, for which the eigenvalues of B sum to 0.

**L2.H2** · NAT · 2 marks. u ∈ ℝ⁵ is a unit vector and A = 3I − uuᵀ. Find det A.

**L2.H3** · MCQ · 2 marks. A is a non-zero n × n matrix with Aᵏ = 0 for some k. Which must be true? (A) trace(A) = 0 (B) A = 0 (C) A is invertible (D) A is diagonalisable

**L2.H4** · MSQ · 2 marks. A is 3 × 3 with eigenvalues 1, 2 and 3. Which statements about adj(A), the adjugate of A, are correct? (A) The eigenvalues of adj(A) are 6, 3 and 2 (B) det A = 6 (C) trace(adj(A)) = 11 (D) det(adj(A)) = 6

**L2.H5** · MSQ · 2 marks. A and B are n × n real matrices. Which are always true? (A) The eigenvalues of A + B are sums of eigenvalues of A and B (B) A and Aᵀ have the same eigenvectors (C) AB and BA have the same eigenvalues (D) If A has n distinct eigenvalues, A is diagonalisable

**L2.H6** · NAT · 2 marks. A two-state Markov chain has transition matrix P = [[0.9, 0.1], [0.5, 0.5]] (rows sum to 1). Its stationary distribution π satisfies πP = π with π₁ + π₂ = 1, so π is a left eigenvector for the eigenvalue 1. Find π₁. (Round off to two decimal places)

**L2.H7** · NAT · 2 marks. Find the dimension of the eigenspace of [[2, 1, 0], [0, 2, 0], [0, 0, 2]] for the eigenvalue 2.

## Set L3: Special matrices, quadratic forms and projections

### Easy

**L3.E1** · MCQ · 1 mark. The determinant of a real orthogonal matrix is always in (A) {0} (B) {−1, 1} (C) {1} (D) ℝ

**L3.E2** · NAT · 1 mark. u ∈ ℝ⁴ is a unit vector and P = uuᵀ. Find trace(P).

**L3.E3** · MCQ · 1 mark. Every eigenvalue of an idempotent matrix is (A) 1 (B) 1 or −1 (C) any real number (D) 0 or 1

**L3.E4** · MCQ · 1 mark. For A = [[2, 1], [1, 3]] and x = (1, 1)ᵀ, xᵀAx is (A) 5 (B) 6 (C) 7 (D) 8

**L3.E5** · MSQ · 1 mark. P is an orthogonal projection matrix (P² = P and Pᵀ = P). Which are correct? (A) P is always invertible (B) Pᵀ = P (C) P² = I (D) I − P is also an orthogonal projection

**L3.E6** · NAT · 1 mark. Q is a 3 × 3 orthogonal matrix and ‖x‖ = 5. Find ‖Qx‖.

**L3.E7** · MCQ · 1 mark. The quadratic form of [[2, 0], [0, −3]] is (A) positive definite (B) indefinite (C) negative definite (D) positive semidefinite

**L3.E8** · NAT · 1 mark. Find the rank of the 6 × 6 centring matrix I − (1/6)jjᵀ, where j is the all-ones vector.

### Medium

**L3.M1** · NAT · 2 marks. Find the first component of the orthogonal projection of b = (3, 4) onto the line spanned by (1, 1). (Round off to one decimal place)

**L3.M2** · MCQ · 2 marks. The matrix [[4, 2], [2, 1]] is (A) positive definite (B) indefinite (C) positive semidefinite but singular (D) negative definite

**L3.M3** · NAT · 2 marks. A is a 5 × 2 matrix of rank 2 and P = A(AᵀA)⁻¹Aᵀ. Find trace(P).

**L3.M4** · MSQ · 2 marks. x₁, …, x₄ are orthonormal vectors in ℝ⁸ and P = x₁x₁ᵀ + … + x₄x₄ᵀ. Which are correct? (A) det P = 1 (B) P² = P (C) rank(P) = 8 (D) Every eigenvalue of I − P is 0 or 1

**L3.M5** · MSQ · 2 marks. A = [[3, 1], [1, 3]]. Which statements are correct? (A) A is indefinite (B) The maximum is attained at x = (1, 1)/√2 (C) The maximum of xᵀAx over unit vectors x is 4 (D) The minimum of xᵀAx over unit vectors x is 2

**L3.M6** · MCQ · 2 marks. A real n × n matrix A satisfies ‖Ax‖ = ‖x‖ for every x. Then (A) A must be symmetric (B) A = I (C) A must be orthogonal (D) 0 is an eigenvalue of A

**L3.M7** · MSQ · 2 marks. M = [[A, B], [0, D]] where A is 2 × 2 with det A = 3 and D is 3 × 3 with det D = −2. Which statements are correct? (A) Every eigenvalue of A is an eigenvalue of M (B) M is invertible (C) det M = det A + det D (D) det M = −6

**L3.M8** · NAT · 2 marks. Find the minimum of q(x) = x₁² + 4x₁x₂ + x₂² over unit vectors x.

**L3.M9** · MCQ · 2 marks. X is a real matrix with linearly independent columns and G = XᵀX. Then (A) G may be singular (B) G is orthogonal (C) G has a negative eigenvalue (D) G is positive definite

### Hard

**L3.H1** · NAT · 2 marks. C is the 10 × 10 centring matrix and x = (1, 2, …, 10)ᵀ. Find xᵀCx. (Round off to one decimal place)

**L3.H2** · NAT · 2 marks. u ∈ ℝ⁴ is a unit vector and H = I − 2uuᵀ. Find det H.

**L3.H3** · MCQ · 2 marks. The quadratic form 2x² + 2y² + 2z² + 2xy + 2yz + 2zx is (A) positive definite (B) positive semidefinite but not definite (C) indefinite (D) negative definite

**L3.H4** · NAT · 2 marks. Find the determinant of [[2, 1, 1], [1, 2, 0], [1, 0, 2]].

**L3.H5** · MSQ · 2 marks. P is a real n × n matrix with P² = P (not necessarily symmetric). Which are always true? (A) rank(P) = trace(P) (B) P is diagonalisable (C) P is an orthogonal projection (D) I − P = 0

**L3.H6** · MSQ · 2 marks. b = (1, 2, 4) is projected onto the column space of A = [[1, 0], [1, 1], [1, 2]]. Which statements are correct? (A) The residual b − Pb is orthogonal to both columns of A (B) The residual is zero (C) The projection is (5/6, 7/3, 23/6) (D) The least-squares line through (0, 1), (1, 2), (2, 4) is y = 5/6 + 1.5x

**L3.H7** · NAT · 2 marks. u and v are orthonormal vectors in ℝ⁵ and A = I + uuᵀ + vvᵀ. Find det A.
