# How to use the warm-up
%Twelve one-hour Saturday sessions, 7 November 2026 to 23 January 2027, to start GATE without slowing placement preparation

Your placement plan runs on weekdays until late January. This book keeps GATE moving at the cost of one hour each Saturday. Each session has a short reading from the GATE DA Guide, Volume 1, then eight questions: four on probability basics (the largest section of the paper) and four on General Aptitude (15 marks that every candidate can earn).

| Part of the hour | What to do |
|---|---|
| 15 minutes | Read the guide sections named for the session |
| 35 minutes | Answer the eight questions without notes; a basic calculator is allowed |
| 10 minutes | Check the answers at the back and redo anything you missed |

All questions carry 1 mark. MCQs have one correct option; NAT answers are typed numbers.

| Session | Saturday | Probability topic | Guide reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 7 Nov 2026 | Counting: sum and product rules | §1.1 |
| 2 | 14 Nov | Permutations and combinations | §1.1 |

| 5 | 5 Dec | Total probability and Bayes | §1.3 |
| 6 | 12 Dec | Independence | §1.3 |
| 7 | 19 Dec | Random variables and expectation | §2.1–2.2 |

# The sessions

## Session 1: Counting

**1.1** · NAT. A person owns 3 shirts and 4 pairs of trousers. How many different outfits of one shirt and one pair of trousers are possible?

**1.2** · MCQ. How many three-letter codes can be made from the 26 letters if letters may repeat? (A) 17,576 (B) 15,600 (C) 2,600 (D) 78

**1.3** · NAT. How many distinct arrangements of the letters of LEVEL are there?

**1.4** · MCQ. C(8, 2) equals (A) 56 (B) 28 (C) 16 (D) 64

**1.5** · MCQ. Which word is closest in meaning to "abundant"? (A) Scarce (B) Rare (C) Plentiful (D) Small

**1.6** · MCQ. She is good ________ mathematics. (A) in (B) at (C) on (D) with

**1.7** · NAT. Find 25% of 80 plus 10% of 150.

**1.8** · MCQ. The next number in 1, 4, 9, 16, … is (A) 20 (B) 24 (C) 36 (D) 25

## Session 2: Permutations and combinations

**2.1** · NAT. How many ways can gold, silver and bronze medals go to 6 runners?

**2.2** · MCQ. A committee of 3 is chosen from 5 men and 4 women. How many committees have exactly one woman? (A) 60 (B) 80 (C) 30 (D) 40

**2.3** · NAT. How many solutions does x + y + z = 6 have in non-negative integers?

**2.4** · MCQ. In how many ways can 6 people sit at a round table (rotations are the same)? (A) 720 (B) 120 (C) 60 (D) 24

**2.5** · MCQ. Which word is most nearly opposite to "transparent"? (A) Opaque (B) Clear (C) Visible (D) Bright

**2.6** · MCQ. Each of the boys ________ a bicycle. (A) have (B) having (C) has (D) are having

**2.7** · NAT. Find the average of 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20.

**2.8** · MCQ. A is B's brother and B is C's mother. A is C's (A) uncle (B) father (C) brother (D) cousin

**3.3** · NAT. A card is drawn from a standard deck. Find the probability that it is a heart or a king. (Round off to two decimal places)

**3.4** · MCQ. Two fair coins are tossed. The probability of exactly one head is (A) 1/4 (B) 1/3 (C) 3/4 (D) 1/2

**3.8** · MCQ. The next letter in A, D, G, J, … is (A) M (B) L (C) N (D) K

## Session 4: Conditional probability

**4.2** · MCQ. Two dice are thrown. Given that the first shows 3, the probability that the sum is 7 is (A) 1/36 (B) 1/6 (C) 1/3 (D) 1/12

**4.3** · NAT. Two cards are drawn without replacement. Find the probability that both are aces. (Round off to four decimal places)

**4.4** · MCQ. A family has two children, and at least one is a boy. The probability that both are boys is (A) 1/2 (B) 1/4 (C) 1/3 (D) 2/3

**4.5** · MCQ. Book : Library :: Painting : ________ (A) Artist (B) Brush (C) Canvas (D) Gallery

**4.6** · MCQ. The news ________ good. (A) are (B) is (C) were (D) have been

**4.7** · NAT. ₹2000 is divided in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5. What is the smallest share in rupees?

**4.8** · MCQ. Facing north, you turn right twice and then left once. You now face (A) east (B) west (C) north (D) south

## Session 5: Total probability and Bayes

**5.1** · NAT. Bag A holds 3 red and 2 blue balls; bag B holds 1 red and 4 blue. A bag is chosen at random and a ball drawn. Find P(red). (Round off to one decimal place)

**5.2** · NAT. In the setting of 5.1, the ball is red. Find the probability that it came from bag A. (Round off to two decimal places)

**5.3** · MCQ. A disease affects 1% of people. A test is positive for 99% of the sick and 5% of the healthy. P(sick | positive) is closest to (A) 0.99 (B) 0.5 (C) 0.17 (D) 0.05

**5.4** · MCQ. Bayes' theorem states that P(A | B) equals (A) P(A)P(B) (B) P(B | A) (C) P(A ∩ B)P(B) (D) P(B | A)P(A)/P(B)

**5.5** · MCQ. Which word is spelt correctly? (A) Recieve (B) Receive (C) Receeve (D) Receve

**5.6** · MCQ. Order the sentences. P: So she carried an umbrella. Q: The sky was dark that morning. R: Still, it did not rain all day. (A) QPR (B) PQR (C) RQP (D) QRP

**5.7** · NAT. ₹2000 grows to ₹2600 in 3 years at simple interest. Find the annual rate in percent.

**5.8** · MCQ. Which number is the odd one out: 3, 5, 7, 12, 13? (A) 3 (B) 7 (C) 13 (D) 12

## Session 6: Independence

**6.3** · MCQ. Two mutually exclusive events, each with positive probability, are (A) always dependent (B) always independent (C) independent only if equally likely (D) independent only if their probabilities sum to 1

**6.4** · NAT. Three fair coins are tossed. Find the probability that all three show the same face. (Round off to two decimal places)

**6.5** · MCQ. Neither of the answers ________ correct. (A) are (B) were (C) is (D) have

**6.6** · MCQ. Which word is closest in meaning to "diligent"? (A) Lazy (B) Careless (C) Quick (D) Hard-working

**6.7** · NAT. A price of ₹500 rises by 10% and then by another 10%. Find the new price in rupees.

**6.8** · MCQ. In a code, MOBILE is written NPCJMF. FOLDER is written (A) GPMEFS (B) GPMDFS (C) EPMEFS (D) GOMEFS
