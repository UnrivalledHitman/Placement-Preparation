# How to use this mock-test book
%Three timed mocks of 20 questions in the TCS NQT style, with hints and Python-checked solutions

Each mock has 20 questions. Take it in one sitting with a **25-minute timer**, on paper, without a calculator (the on-screen calculator in TCS iON is basic and slow; practise without it). Mark your answers, then check them against the answer key at the start of each mock's solutions. Read the hint for a wrong answer, try again, and only then read the worked solution. Every answer was recomputed by a Python program that checks the keyed option.

| Mock | Section | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Numerical ability | 20 | 25 min |
| 2 | Reasoning ability | 20 | 25 min |
| 3 | Mixed numerical and reasoning | 20 | 25 min |

**Marking scheme.** Score 1 per correct answer, no negative marks. In the tracker mark ✓ (correct in time), ~ (correct but slow, or after the hint) or ✗ (wrong). A score of 15/20 or more in 25 minutes is a strong NQT foundation performance. Redo every ~ and ✗ after a week.

# Exercises

## Part 1: Mock 1, numerical ability (25 minutes)

**A1.1 · Easy · Numerical · Price rise and consumption.** The price of sugar rises by 25%. By what percentage must a family reduce consumption to keep its expenditure unchanged? (a) 25% (b) 20% (c) 22.5% (d) 18%

**A1.2 · Easy · Numerical · Mark-up and discount.** An article is marked 40% above cost price and sold at a discount of 15%. The profit percentage is (a) 25% (b) 21% (c) 19% (d) 17%

**A1.3 · Easy · Numerical · Combined ratio.** If A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5, then A : B : C is (a) 8 : 12 : 15 (b) 2 : 3 : 5 (c) 6 : 9 : 10 (d) 8 : 12 : 10

**A1.4 · Easy · Numerical · Corrected average.** The average of 10 numbers is 45. One number, 60, was wrongly read as 90. The correct average is (a) 41 (b) 42 (c) 43 (d) 48

**A1.5 · Medium · Numerical · Work with a departure.** A can do a job in 12 days and B in 18 days. They work together for 3 days, then A leaves. In how many days in total is the job finished? (a) 12 (b) 13 (c) 13.5 (d) 15

**A1.6 · Medium · Numerical · Pipes and a leak.** Pipes A and B fill a tank in 20 and 30 minutes; pipe C empties it in 15 minutes. If all three are opened together on an empty tank, it fills in (a) 45 min (b) 50 min (c) 60 min (d) never

**A1.7 · Medium · Numerical · Train and platform.** A 240 m train passes a pole in 12 s. How long does it take to pass a 360 m platform? (a) 24 s (b) 30 s (c) 36 s (d) 18 s

**A1.8 · Easy · Numerical · Speed of the stream.** A boat goes 24 km downstream in 2 hours and the same distance upstream in 3 hours. The speed of the stream is (a) 1 km/h (b) 2 km/h (c) 3 km/h (d) 4 km/h

**A1.9 · Easy · Numerical · Compound interest.** The compound interest on Rs 8 000 at 10% per annum for 2 years, compounded annually, is (a) Rs 1 600 (b) Rs 1 640 (c) Rs 1 680 (d) Rs 1 720

**A1.10 · Easy · Numerical · Alligation.** Rice at Rs 60/kg and Rs 80/kg is mixed to get a mixture worth Rs 75/kg. The ratio of the cheaper to the dearer rice is (a) 1 : 3 (b) 3 : 1 (c) 1 : 2 (d) 2 : 3

**A1.11 · Medium · Numerical · Remainder of a power.** The remainder when 7<sup>100</sup> is divided by 5 is (a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4

**A1.12 · Easy · Numerical · LCM.** The least number exactly divisible by 12, 15, 20 and 27 is (a) 270 (b) 540 (c) 1 080 (d) 360

**A1.13 · Easy · Numerical · Selecting a team.** In how many ways can 2 boys and 2 girls be chosen from 5 boys and 4 girls? (a) 40 (b) 60 (c) 120 (d) 90

**A1.14 · Medium · Numerical · Two aces.** Two cards are drawn together from a pack of 52. The probability that both are aces is (a) 1/169 (b) 1/221 (c) 1/26 (d) 2/221

**A1.15 · Easy · Numerical · Three coins.** Three fair coins are tossed. The probability of getting at least two heads is (a) 3/8 (b) 1/2 (c) 5/8 (d) 1/4

**A1.16 · Medium · Numerical · Ages.** A father is three times as old as his son. In 10 years he will be twice as old as his son. The sum of their present ages is (a) 40 (b) 44 (c) 48 (d) 36

**A1.17 · Medium · Numerical · Clock angle.** The angle between the hands of a clock at 9:15 is (a) 172.5° (b) 187.5° (c) 165° (d) 180°

**A1.18 · Medium · Numerical · Day of the week.** 1 January 2026 is a Thursday. What day is 1 January 2030? (a) Monday (b) Tuesday (c) Wednesday (d) Thursday

**A1.19 · Easy · Numerical · Cylinder volume.** The volume of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm (π = 22/7) is (a) 1 440 cm³ (b) 1 540 cm³ (c) 1 640 cm³ (d) 770 cm³

**A1.20 · Easy · Numerical · Median.** The median of 3, 7, 9, 2, 8, 6 is (a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 6.5 (d) 5.8

**A2.5 · Easy · Reasoning · Letter coding.** If TABLE is written as UBCMF, how is CHAIR written? (a) DIBJS (b) DGBJS (c) BIBJS (d) DIBKS

**A2.6 · Easy · Reasoning · Number coding.** If CAT = 24 (the sum of letter positions), then DOG = (a) 24 (b) 25 (c) 26 (d) 27

**A2.7 · Medium · Reasoning · Family tree.** A is B's sister. C is B's mother. D is C's father. E is D's mother. How is A related to D? (a) Granddaughter (b) Daughter (c) Great-granddaughter (d) Niece

**A2.8 · Medium · Reasoning · Photograph.** Pointing to a photograph of a girl, a man says, "Her mother is the only daughter of my father." The man is the girl's (a) father (b) brother (c) maternal uncle (d) cousin

**A2.9 · Easy · Reasoning · Distance.** Sita walks 6 km north and then 8 km east. How far is she from the start? (a) 14 km (b) 10 km (c) 12 km (d) 2 km

**A2.10 · Easy · Reasoning · Turns.** Facing east, a man turns left, then left again, then right. Which direction does he face? (a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West

**A2.11 · Medium · Reasoning · Syllogism I.** All roses are flowers. No flower is a tree. Conclusions: I. No rose is a tree. II. Some trees are roses. (a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**A2.12 · Medium · Reasoning · Syllogism II.** Some cats are dogs. All dogs are pets. Conclusions: I. Some cats are pets. II. All pets are cats. (a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows

**A2.13 · Hard · Reasoning · Circular seating.** P, Q, R, S, T sit around a circular table facing the centre. Q is to the immediate right of P. R is not next to P. S is to the immediate left of T. Who is to the immediate left of P? (a) R (b) S (c) T (d) Q

**A2.14 · Medium · Reasoning · Data sufficiency.** Is the integer x even? I. x² is even. II. x + 3 is odd. (a) I alone (b) II alone (c) Both together (d) Either alone

**A2.15 · Easy · Reasoning · Rank.** In a class of 40, Ravi is 12th from the top. His rank from the bottom is (a) 28 (b) 29 (c) 30 (d) 27

**A2.16 · Medium · Reasoning · Ordering.** A is older than B. C is younger than B. D is older than A. E is younger than C. Who is the youngest? (a) B (b) C (c) E (d) A

**A2.17 · Easy · Reasoning · Analogy.** 3 : 27 :: 5 : ? (a) 25 (b) 125 (c) 15 (d) 75

**A2.18 · Medium · Reasoning · Mirror image.** A clock shows 3:40. What time does its mirror image show? (a) 8:20 (b) 9:20 (c) 8:40 (d) 7:20

**A2.19 · Medium · Reasoning · Number pattern.** In the sequence 5 3 5 7 2 3 5 7 5 3 5 1 3 5 7, how many 5s are immediately preceded by 3 and immediately followed by 7? (a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4

**A2.20 · Medium · Reasoning · Painted cube.** A cube painted on all faces is cut into 64 equal small cubes. How many small cubes have exactly two faces painted? (a) 16 (b) 24 (c) 32 (d) 8
